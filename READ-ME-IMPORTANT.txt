## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Install Python** – Download from [python.org](https://python.org) and make sure to check **"Add Python to PATH"**.
2. **Open Command Prompt** in the folder where `sniper.py` is located.
3. **Run the script** (see Code Block 2 below for the exact command).
4. **Select `[1]` Start Sniper** from the menu.

---

## 🧾 How to Get Your Discord Token (Step-by-Step)

1. Open **Discord** in your **browser** (not the desktop app).
2. Press **F12** to open Developer Tools.
3. Click the **Network** tab at the top.
4. In the filter bar, type:/api
5. Press **Ctrl + R** to refresh the page.
6. Click on any request that appears (e.g., `science`, `messages`, or `users/@me`).
7. In the new panel on the right, scroll down to **Request Headers**.
8. Look for the line that says: authorization 
9. Copy **only** the long string after `authorization:`.
10. Paste it into `tokens.txt` — one per line.

> ⚠️ **Never share your token** — it gives full access to your account.
